# Viral reel breakdown (livestock / feed / farm niche, Pakistan)

Use when the owner sends a reel that went viral (link or screen recording) and asks WHY it worked
and WHAT similar reels to make. Facebook links usually need a login: ask for a screen recording
(Control Centre ⏺) and keep the views / likes / comments / shares visible in it if possible.

## 1. Get and study it
- `python3 tools/fetch.py "<link>" --project <name> --to reference` (or copy the recording into Reference/).
- Look at `sheet.jpg` + frames at 0.0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 3 s and every cut; transcribe it (`transcribe.py --language ur`).
- Note the visible numbers (views, likes, comments, shares, saves) and the top comments if shown.

## 2. Score it (write the answers into Reference/<name>/breakdown.md)
1. **Hook (0–2 s)**: what stops the thumb? (shock visual, big number, question, conflict, animal close-up,
   money, before/after, "log kehte hain…", famous farm/customer, price). Words on screen in frame 1?
2. **Story**: the one promise of the video, and is it paid off? (result, reveal, transformation, delivery reached).
3. **Emotion**: pride (qurbani janwar), trust, surprise, humour, aspiration (big farm), FOMO (stock khatam).
4. **Retention tricks**: cuts every 1–2 s, open loop ("aakhir mein dekhna"), counting list, progress, reveal at end.
5. **Proof**: real customer, real farm, numbers (kg, litres, weight gain shown, bags), city names, faces.
6. **Format**: talking head / POV / dispatch / unboxing / before-after / tips list / reaction / trend audio.
7. **Captions & graphics**: style, size, colour, what is written vs spoken.
8. **Sound**: trending audio? voice energy, SFX density, music drop on the reveal.
9. **Length & loop**: total seconds; does the end flow back into the start?
10. **CTA & comments bait**: question that makes people comment ("aap ka janwar kitne kg ka hai?"), share trigger.
11. **Timing**: season (Eid-ul-Adha, mandi season, winter milk drop), posting time if known.

## 3. Turn it into reels for the owner
Give 3–5 concrete ideas the owner can shoot with their own shop/customers, each as:
`Title · Hook line (first 2 s, Roman Urdu) · Shots to film (3–5) · On-screen text · Ending/CTA · which of our styles`.
Keep what made the original work (format, hook type, emotion), change the content to the owner's products,
dispatches and customers. Never copy their footage, music or exact script. No medical / weight-gain
claims that the bag or the owner didn't make. Also see guides/shop-story-reels.md.

## 4. Remember
Append the winning patterns to the "Learned patterns" list below and commit, so every next idea uses them.

  Captions only (style plain-captions) and a small persistent product strip. Full notes:
  Video Edits/Reference/viral-1/breakdown.md
- (TikTok @ktvet1 audit, 2026-11-19, last 250 posts) Weekly median views ~1,000 until late Oct, then 765 → 687
  → 412. ALL top posts (42.7k, 37k, 21k, 15k, 13k, 11.7k, 10.6k) are short (11–26 s) ANIMAL clips (bachra /
  bakra) with only hashtags, no sales caption. Recent posts are product/dispatch ads with long captions,
  keyword lists ("Veterinary products Pakistan, wholesale veterinary pharmacy…"), medicine names
  (Ivermectin, vaccines), "order ke liye inbox", 4–6 posts a day. TikTok rules: lead with the ANIMAL, keep the
  product secondary, caption = 1 line + 3–5 hashtags, no medicine/drug promotion, no keyword stuffing,
  max 1–2 posts/day. Facebook can keep the full sales copy.
- (TikTok analytics, "Uncle / Mega-VMix" 10 s, 2026-11-19) 96% of views came from For You, so it was NOT
  ineligible, but avg watch 5.1 s, 15% watched full, "most viewers stopped at 0:01". Cause: frame 1 was a
  blurry whip-pan and the first caption was still typing in, so nothing readable/interesting at 0:00-0:01.
  RULE for TikTok: frame 1 = the clearest, most interesting shot (a face, an animal, the reveal) moved to the
  front (cold open), and the hook text is FULLY on screen at 0.0 s (no typing / no fade on the first line).
  Cut the shaky/blurry first seconds.
