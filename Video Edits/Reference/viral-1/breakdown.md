# Viral breakdown: Buttery Milk "Anda More" dispatch (owner's CapCut reel)
Link: https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1Q7Jc83DSu/ · 11.5 s · 2 shots · handheld · -14 LUFS (loud)

Speech: "Anda Mor ka ilaqa hai Karachi mein · janwaron ka wazan tezi se barhane wala · protein aur
butter fat wala doodh hai bhai · aur yeh Shakeel bhai ka doodh ja raha hai · do bag bhai, brand hai brand waqai"

## Why it worked
1. **Hook = a place name people know** ("Anda Mor, Karachi") in frame 1, typed big: local viewers stop.
2. **Real & raw**: bags tied on a motorcycle, a real customer carrying them. Feels like proof, not an ad.
3. **One clear promise** in 3 s: "janwaron ka wazan tezi se barhane wala doodh". The benefit is the hook.
4. **Captions are the design**: only the key word, huge, white caps + red script, typing in. Easy to
   read with sound off; only 1–2 words at a time = fast rhythm.
5. **Persistent product strip** at the bottom (bag + slogan) the whole time = brand recall without blocking.
6. **Short (11 s) and loops**: ends on "brand hai brand", which flows back into the start: rewatches.
7. **Social proof + pride**: customer's name ("Shakeel bhai"), "do bag", "brand hai brand waqai".
8. **Loud, energetic voice** (-14 LUFS), no music needed.

end on a short punch line that loops, and ask a question in the post text ("aap ka area konsa hai?").
