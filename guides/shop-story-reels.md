# Shop story reels (voice-over stories that get views)

The owner runs a livestock supplement shop (Buttery Milk, Heavy Gold, Mega-Tox ...). Cattle,
dairy and goat farmers visit; the owner tells a short story in voice-over. Use these formulas
when planning or scripting a reel, and suggest one to the owner when they share footage.

## The rules that drive views
1. **Hook in 2 seconds with the farmer's PROBLEM**, not the product:
   "Bhains ne doodh kam kar diya?", "Bakra Eid se pehle wazan nahi barh raha?"
2. **A real person**: "Yeh Maahid bhai hain, Sialkot se aaye hain..." Faces, names and cities
   build trust and make farmers from that city share it.
3. **One product, one promise** per reel. Say the key fact once, clearly
   ("34% protein", "1 kg powder = 10 litre doodh"), shown on screen as a card.
4. **Proof over claims**: the customer's own words, before/after of the animal, the bag
   being loaded, repeat customers ("teesri dafa le ja rahe hain").
5. **End with one clear action**: "Comment mein PRICE likhein" / "DM karein" + city delivery.
6. **15–35 seconds**, fast cuts every 2–3 s, captions always on (most watch muted).
7. **Series** make people come back: "Farmer of the week", "1 minute farm tip", "Bakra Eid
   countdown – Day 7".

## Story formulas (pick one per video)
- **Problem → Solution → Proof**: farmer's problem (5 s) → product + how to use (10 s) →
  customer says it worked / animal shot (8 s) → CTA.
- **Customer journey**: "Maahid bhai Karachi se Sialkot maal le ja rahe hain" → why they chose
  it → map card → what they say → CTA. (Map + name strip work very well.)
- **Myth buster**: "Log kehte hain sirf chara kaafi hai... galat!" → the fact → the product.
- **Expert tip**: 1 useful tip (mastitis signs, heat stress, deworming time) → product only at
  the end. Builds trust; farmers save and share tips.
- **Seasonal**: Eid / summer / winter: "Garmi mein doodh kyun kam hota hai?" → tip → product.
- **Numbers**: "20 kg bag, 200 litre doodh" style calculation on screen (cards).
- **Behind the scenes**: unloading stock, packing orders, the shop at night: "Sialkot ka
  order ja raha hai" — shows a busy, trusted shop.

## Voice-over tips
- Talk to ONE farmer ("aap ki bhains..."), short sentences, local words.
- Record close to the phone in a quiet corner; one take per sentence is fine.
- Pause half a second between ideas: the edit cuts there.
- Say numbers and names clearly (they become cards and captions).

## Shots to collect at the shop (every time a farmer visits)
Bag close-up (front label), farmer with the bag, loading the bag on bike/truck, the animal at
the farm if possible (ask them to send a 10 s clip), the farmer saying one sentence on camera,
city/vehicle shot for the map card.
