# Style: plain-captions ("PLAIN VIDEO")

Trigger: the owner says **"plain video"**. Learned 2026-11-18 from the owner's own viral CapCut reel
(Buttery Milk, "ANDA More / WEIGHT Gainer / Protien & Butter Fat / Shakeel Bhai"):
https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1Q7Jc83DSu/ (sheet: reference-sheet.jpg, our version: preview.jpg).

- Raw handheld footage, almost no graphics: the CAPTIONS are the design.
- Caption template `script-mix`: 1–2 words per line, centre (y 0.62). Plain words in heavy white
  caps (Montserrat Black, soft drop shadow); the key word in a big red brush script (Yellowtail) with a
  thick white outline, tilted, sitting lower and overlapping. Every word TYPES in left→right behind a
  thin white cursor bar. Mark the script word with `*word*` in captions.txt (usually the 2nd word:
  "anda *More*", "weight *Gainer*", "Shakeel *Bhai*", "*Protein* & *Butter Fat*").
- Accent = the product's red/brand colour (default #E8202A).
- SFX: soft iPhone `keys` typing under each line, `whoosh_fast` on script words, plus impact on the
  hook and pop/ding on the product/customer name (all under the voice).
- Optional (as in the reference): a persistent bottom product strip (product cutout + slogan), only if
  the owner asks.
- Everything else from the house look: 4K, bright crisp cinematic grade, safe zone, caption review first.
